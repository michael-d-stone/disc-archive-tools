# Roadmap

## DVD-Video reliability

### Archive rechecks — complete

Five existing archives were rechecked against their recorded sources: Frasier
season 1 disc 1, Massive Asses 3, Asa Akira Is Pure discs 1 and 2, and Squirt
Queens 11. All passed the current inventory, receipt, checksum, and media-probe
checks. The original manifests were preserved and timestamped results saved.
Passing these checks does not establish full content preservation or playback.

### Investigate disc 2 galleries — open

Asa Akira Is Pure disc 2 titles 7–17 have far fewer extracted video packets and
chapter markers than their authored durations and chapter counts suggest. They
appear to be still-image galleries. Confirm what a DVD player displays,
investigate why the DVD reader returns so few packets, and determine how to
preserve the images and their order. Compare the preserved result with the
source and make verification flag comparable discrepancies. Gallery completeness
is unverified despite the passing archive check. Keep the original ISO and
extraction while investigating.

## Blu-ray — next format

After the DVD-Video reliability work, investigate Blu-ray source structures,
available tools, and representative test discs. Use those findings to define an
extraction and verification workflow and its supported capabilities.

The milestone is complete when the workflow is implemented, tested against
representative sources, and its capabilities and limitations are documented.
